# Baro Studio — WhatsApp Message Templates

Copy-paste ready templates for every stage of the order journey.
Replace `[PLACEHOLDERS]` with the actual values.

## 1. Order Received Confirmation

Send immediately when a new order comes in.

```
Hi [CUSTOMER NAME] 🤍

Thank you for your order!

Here's a summary:
• Item: [ITEM NAME]
• Size: [SIZE]
• Colour: [COLOUR]
• Qty: [QTY]
• Amount: PKR [TOTAL]

Your Order ID is *[ORDER ID]* — please save this for reference.

To confirm your order, please complete payment within *24 hours*:

💳 JazzCash: [JAZZ CASH NUMBER]
💳 Easypaisa: [EASYPAISA NUMBER]
🏦 Bank: [BANK NAME] | [ACCOUNT NUMBER] | [ACCOUNT TITLE]

Please send a screenshot of the payment receipt once done.

— Baro Studio
```
