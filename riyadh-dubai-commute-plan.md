## 1. Flights

### Route & Airlines

Four airlines operate **non-stop** DXB ↔ RUH (flight time ~2 hrs):

| Airline | Weekly Flights | Type | Notes |
|---------|---------------|------|-------|
| **flynas (XY)** | ~43 | Budget | Cheapest option |
| **flydubai (FZ)** | ~28 | Budget | Good availability |
| Saudi Arabian Airlines (SV) | ~51 | Full-service | More expensive |
| Emirates (EK) | ~22 | Premium | Priciest |

**Stick to flynas or flydubai for lowest fares.**

---

### Leg 1: Dubai (DXB) → Riyadh (RUH) — Sunday Morning Arrival

> Time zones: Dubai = UTC+4 | Riyadh = UTC+3  
> Riyadh is **1 hour behind** Dubai, so a 2-hour flight departs Dubai and arrives ~1 hour later (local time).

| Depart Dubai | Arrive Riyadh | Notes |
|-------------|--------------|-------|
| ~6:00 AM | ~7:00 AM | Early morning — full work day ahead |
| ~7:00 AM | ~8:00 AM | ✅ **Recommended** — civilised start |
| ~9:00 AM | ~10:00 AM | Late morning — loses half work morning |
| 12:40 AM (Sat night) | ~1:40 AM | Red-eye, budget, rest at hotel |
| 1:55 AM (Sat night) | ~2:55 AM | flynas red-eye option |

**Best pick:** A 6:00–7:00 AM Sunday departure from Dubai lands you in Riyadh by 7:00–8:00 AM — ready for the work week.

**Typical one-way price (flynas/flydubai, booked 3–4 weeks ahead):** AED 200–320

---

### Leg 2: Riyadh (RUH) → Dubai (DXB) — Wednesday Night

| Depart Riyadh | Arrive Dubai | Notes |
|--------------|-------------|-------|
| ~6:00 PM | ~9:00 PM | Evening — arrive home at a reasonable hour |
| ~8:35 PM | ~11:35 PM | ✅ **Recommended flynas flight** — last viable flight |
| ~9:00 PM+ | ~12:00 AM+ | Very late, avoid if possible |

**Best pick:** The **~8:35 PM flynas flight from Riyadh** arrives Dubai at ~11:35 PM — you're home Wednesday night without losing the working day.

**Typical one-way price (flynas/flydubai, booked 3–4 weeks ahead):** AED 200–320

## 2. Riyadh Accommodation (Sun night – Wed night)

You'll need ~3–4 nights per trip. Two approaches:

### Option A: Monthly Furnished Apartment (Recommended for Weekly Commute)

If going **every week**, a monthly furnished apartment is cheaper and more comfortable than Airbnb.

| Area | SAR/month | AED/month (approx) | AED/year |
|------|----------|--------------------|----------|
| Al Olaya / Al Malqa (North, expat-friendly) | SAR 4,000–5,000 | AED 3,920–4,900 | AED 47,040–58,800 |
| Al Muruj / Al Nakheel (Central) | SAR 3,500–4,500 | AED 3,430–4,410 | AED 41,160–52,920 |
| Al Sahafah / Al Narjis | SAR 3,000–4,000 | AED 2,940–3,920 | AED 35,280–47,040 |

> **Recommended:** Al Muruj or Al Nakheel area — central, close to most business districts, SAR 3,500–4,000/month (~AED 41,000–47,000/year).

**Note:** You pay for all 12 months even though you're only there half the time — but this is still cheaper than Airbnb at this frequency and gives you stability (store clothes, toiletries, etc. there permanently).

---

### Option B: Airbnb / Short-Term Rental (Better for Bi-Weekly Commute)

| Nights/trip | Nightly rate | Cost/trip | Annual (26 trips) |
|------------|-------------|----------|------------------|
| 4 nights | AED 400 (~$110) | AED 1,600 | AED 41,600 |
| 3 nights | AED 400 | AED 1,200 | AED 31,200 |

> **Tip:** Look for Airbnbs or Gathern (Saudi Airbnb equivalent) listings that offer weekly/monthly discounts. Some will negotiate 20–30% off for recurring bookings.

## 5. Practical Tips

### Flights
- **Book on Tuesdays or Wednesdays** — historically cheapest booking days
- Set up **Google Flights price alerts** for DXB→RUH and RUH→DXB
- Fly **carry-on only** with flynas/flydubai — saves AED 50–100 each way
- **flynas promo fares** can drop to AED 99–150 one-way — grab them when they appear
- The DXB→RUH route is highly competitive with 140+ flights/week — you rarely need to pay full price

### Riyadh Stay
- If on a monthly apartment, leave essentials there (clothes, toiletries, chargers) — travel light
- Riyadh Uber/Careem is inexpensive; airport to city ~SAR 50–80 (~AED 50–80)
- Many Riyadh restaurants deliver via Jahez/HungerStation — dining costs manageable

### Dubai Base
- Ensure apartment is near **Dubai Metro** (Red Line) or within easy reach of DXB Terminal 1/2 (flynas/flydubai depart from Terminal 2)
- Leave for airport **90 minutes before** Sunday morning departure (lighter traffic early Sunday)
